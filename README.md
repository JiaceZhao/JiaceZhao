### About

Incoming undergraduate at **UC Berkeley**. I work at the intersection of embodied AI, computer-using agents, reinforcement learning, and robot control—moving between mechanical systems, data, models, and interfaces.

I like building things that are technically serious but still feel human to use.

<br>

### Selected work

|  | Project | What I made | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | **[Adaptive 6-DOF Robotic Arm](https://www.jc-zhao.com/projects/adaptive-robotic-arm)** | A low-cost robotic arm with custom electronics, CAN, ROS, depth perception, and YOLOv10. | **55.39%** lower tracking RMSE than PID · **0.98 mAP@0.5** |
